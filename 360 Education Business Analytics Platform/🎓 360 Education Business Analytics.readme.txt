🎓 360 Education Business Analytics Platform
📌 Project Overview

This project is an End-to-End Business Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI for a multi-branch education institute.

The dashboard enables management to monitor admissions, revenue, lead conversions, placements, and fee collection through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

🚀 Business Problem

Educational institutions receive thousands of student inquiries from multiple marketing channels such as:

LinkedIn
Website
Walk-ins
Referrals
YouTube
Instagram

The management struggled to analyze:

Revenue
Admissions
Placements
Marketing performance
Fee Collection

This project solves these challenges through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

📌 Key Insights
Executive Dashboard
Karve Nagar generated the highest revenue.
Software Testing achieved the highest placement rate.
Total revenue reached 66M.
Placement rate stood at 34.85%.

Sales Dashboard
LinkedIn generated the highest quality leads.
Effective Call Rate reached 39.34%.
Lead Conversion Rate remained 31.90%.

Placement Dashboard
Software Testing achieved the highest placement success.
Capgemini hired the highest number of students.
697 students were placed.

Financial Dashboard
Collection Efficiency reached 79.45%.
Cash remained the preferred payment mode.
Total collected fees reached 52M.

💡 Business Recommendations
Increase investment in LinkedIn marketing.
Improve counselor performance through training.
Focus on low-performing branches.
Reduce pending fee collections using automated reminders.
Strengthen placement partnerships with top recruiters.
